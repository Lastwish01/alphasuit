How it works

From raw candles to a watch-list

1 · Scan

Every trading night, after the close, we scan the day's closed candles for all NSE/BSE stocks. Crypto is scanned every night too.

2 · Qualify

Illiquid and suspended stocks are filtered out. Each pattern is scored 0–100 on geometry, volume and trend context.

3 · Study

Open the chart with the detected pattern lines drawn in, see its status (forming or breakout) and score, and study how it develops.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All scanners, patterns and charts are free for everyone, with no account needed.

Is this investment advice?

No. Alphasuit is an educational screening tool that highlights chart structures. Always do your own analysis.

When is data updated?

Indian stocks and crypto are scanned once every night (about 10 PM IST), so fresh results are ready before the market opens. The scanner page always shows the time of the last scan.
